# Privacy Policy for Convertin

**Last Updated:** November 10, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026  

Convertin ("we", "our", or "the application") is a free and open-source desktop file and media conversion utility developed by Naufaal Muhammad. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users.

---

## 1. 100% Local & Offline Processing
Convertin operates **entirely offline** on your local machine:
- **No File Uploads:** Files, documents, images, audio, or video selected for conversion are processed solely on your local device's CPU and GPU. Your files are **never** uploaded, transmitted, or backed up to any cloud service, external server, or third party.
- **No Data Retention:** Converted files are saved directly to your local computer in the folder designated by you. Convertin does not store copies of your files elsewhere.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Data:** Convertin does not require account creation, registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
- **Analytics & Telemetry:** We do not track, log, or collect personal usage statistics, IP addresses, location data, or behavioral analytics.
- **No Third-Party Trackers:** Convertin is completely ad-free and contains no advertising networks or third-party tracking scripts.

---

## 3. Network Connections & Updates
The only network activity Convertin may perform is:
- **Checking for Application Updates:** When you manually click "Check for Updates" or launch the application, Convertin checks the public GitHub API (`https://api.github.com/repos/naufalt/convertin/releases`) to verify whether a newer release version is available. No user-identifiable data or document information is transmitted during this check.

---

## 4. Third-Party Libraries
Convertin utilizes reputable, open-source local processing engines (including FFmpeg, libwebp, and PDF rendering libraries). These engines execute exclusively in your local environment.
